# Шаблон термінового звернення до ООН та ЄСПЛ (Urgent Appeal Form)

> - [1. Інструкція із заповнення та відправки.](#1-інструкція-із-заповнення-та-відправки)
> - [2. Текст звернення англійською мовою (Urgent Appeal).](#2-urgent-appeal-form-english)

---

### 1. Інструкція із заповнення та відправки

- Заповнюється родичами, законними представниками або адвокатом особи, яку позбавлено волі.
- Відправляється електронною поштою на адреси:
  - `hrc-wg-ad@un.org` (Робоча група ООН зі свавільних затримань)
  - `ohchr-hrmmu@un.org` (Моніторингова місія ООН в Україні)
  - `commissioner@coe.int` (Комісар Ради Європи з прав людини)
- Тема листа (Subject):
  `URGENT APPEAL: Arbitrary Detention, Forced Conscription and Enforced Disappearance - [SURNAME NAME]`

---

### 2. Urgent Appeal Form (English)

```text
TO: 
1. UN Working Group on Arbitrary Detention (hrc-wg-ad@un.org)
2. UN Human Rights Monitoring Mission in Ukraine (ohchr-hrmmu@un.org)
3. Council of Europe Commissioner for Human Rights (commissioner@coe.int)

SUBJECT: URGENT APPEAL: Arbitrary Detention, Enforced Disappearance, and 
Forced Conscription of Civilian Citizen in Ukraine

1. IDENTITY OF THE VICTIM:
- Full Name: [ПІБ латиницею, наприклад: IVANOV IVAN IVANOVYCH]
- Date of Birth: [ДД.ММ.РРРР]
- Nationality: Ukrainian
- Legal Status: Civilian (non-military, has never taken military oath)
- Identification / Passport No: [Номер паспорта за наявності]

2. DETAILS OF ARBITRARY DETENTION:
- Date and Time of Abduction: [Дата та час викрадення]
- Location of Abduction: [Вулиця, номер будинку, місто, область]
- Perpetrators: Armed individuals in military uniform without identifying badges 
  (Territorial Center of Recruitment / TCK) acting jointly with National Police officers.
- Vehicle Used: [Марка, колір, номерний знак авто, якщо відомо]
- Current Location of Captivity: [Адреса приміщення ТЦК або номер військової частини]

3. FACTUAL SUMMARY & VIOLATIONS OF INTERNATIONAL LAW:
On [Дата], the victim was unlawfully intercepted on a public street without any judicial warrant, 
subpoena, or legitimate legal cause. 
He was subjected to physical violence, psychological coercion, and had his communication devices 
seized/blocked. No formal detention protocol under Article 208 of the Criminal Procedure Code 
of Ukraine or Article 260 of the Code of Administrative Offenses was executed.

He is being held incommunicado, deprived of access to independent legal counsel and civil healthcare. 
Coercive attempts are currently being made to forge Military Medical Board (VLK) conclusions to 
falsely certify him as fit for military duty and forcibly draft him into armed conflict.

These actions constitute gross violations of fundamental peremptory norms (jus cogens):
- Article 9 of the Universal Declaration of Human Rights (Freedom from arbitrary arrest and detention);
- Articles 7, 9, 10, and 18 of the International Covenant on Civil and Political Rights (ICCPR);
- Articles 3, 5, 6, and 9 of the European Convention on Human Rights (ECHR);
- Articles 3, 19, 28, 29, and 60 of the Constitution of Ukraine.

4. ACTIONS TAKEN AT THE NATIONAL LEVEL:
- Urgent criminal complaints filed with the National Police of Ukraine (102 Hotline Registry EO No: [Номер ЄО]).
- Criminal report submitted to the State Bureau of Investigation (DBR) under Articles 146 (Kidnapping), 
  365 (Excess of authority), and 371 (Unlawful detention) of the Criminal Code of Ukraine.
- Urgent application submitted to the Investigating Judge under Article 206 of the Criminal Procedure 
  Code of Ukraine demanding immediate judicial verification and release.
- National remedies are ineffective due to widespread institutional collusion and ongoing urgency.

5. URGENT RELIEF REQUESTED:
We respectfully request the UN WGAD, HRMMU, and the CoE Commissioner to:
1. Immediately intervene with the Government of Ukraine and the Ministry of Defense regarding 
   this ongoing arbitrary deprivation of liberty;
2. Urgently request the competent authorities to establish the location and physical well-being of the victim;
3. Demand his immediate and unconditional release and protection against forced mobilization into armed conflict;
4. Initiate international documentation of systemic unlawful detentions by mobilization authorities in Ukraine.

Submitted by:

Relationship to victim: [Дружина / Мати / Батько / Адвокат]
Residential Address: [Адреса заявника]
Email: [Ваш email]
Phone: [Ваш телефон]
Date: [Дата подання]
Signature: __________________________
```
