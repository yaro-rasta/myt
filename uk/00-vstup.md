Чернетка

Hello, dear!

It’s been so long since we last saw each other in person — about 20 years. How much we must have changed inside. It would be fascinating to recall what each of us remembers from those days.

I returned to Ukraine on March 8, 2022, leaving behind my job as a solutions architect and my life on the Adriatic coast. I came back to volunteer and help people, to find my true self. Over the past ten years, since 2016, I’ve been actively involved in human rights advocacy, particularly in the field of cannabis. Now, this has evolved into broader human rights work, defending the rights that have been stripped away from people here in Ukraine, especially from menʼs right.

Life here is incredibly interesting and, in many ways, joyful — though it’s nothing like it used to be. I walk barefoot all year round, adapting to the widest possible range of comfort, from living in the forest to enjoying the luxuries of a villa with all modern amenities. Every day can be different here, and over the past four years of this war — though Ukrainian officials hypocritically refuse to call it that, insisting on the term "martial law" — I’ve experienced it all. The truth is, we’re already at war: daily shelling, deaths, destroyed lives, and property. But by calling it "martial law," they deceive us, as it’s economically beneficial for those who have stolen the will of the Ukrainian people.

Here, I’ve even had to be prepared for encounters with the police. Many conflicts, many courts. At one point, I was taken by police, after they torture me and stole my car, to a psychiatric hospital, where I spent six days. So, I’ve gained a lot of new and intense experiences. I also consume fly agaric mushrooms for two months a year to clean up my body, gathering them myself. No more meat and fish for many years. I take good care of my health and haven’t been ill in recent years. I’ve treated my own injuries and continue to heal myself independently. Occasionally, I visit the dentist to clean my teeth or replace old fillings. I’ve resolved my dental issues — no new cavities — and I plan to continue living with healthy teeth. Our diet is a major cause of illness, of course, multiplied by stress destruction.

I’ve rethought so much and continue to do so, especially about how, as humanity, we remain so underdeveloped. We’ve lived for thousands of years in war, boasting about our technological and civilizational progress, yet at the peak of our development, we have nuclear bombs and everything that destroys those who refuse to share our point of view. Imagine if we declared war on people who didn’t share our posts — that’s how absurd real wars are. It’s so bizarre absurd.

Now, I observe global processes manifesting every day at the micro-level in each of us. Conflicts and provocations within families, provoked by our loved ones, teach us to stop the aggression within ourselves. Because for wars to end, the next generation must live without aggression, and each of us is capable of making that happen. Yet, most people carry war within themselves, even in peacetime. They argue, reject one another, and fail to accept that all of us — those we love, as Jesus taught or as my Rastafarian faith reminds me — are essentially one family. We constantly succumb to provocations and aggression, creating wars on both micro and macro levels. Then, one of us reaches a position of power, like a president or a king, or tsar, and begins to wreak havoc on a global scale, destroying millions of lives and the fruits of their labor.

Here, I see people for who they truly are — almost as if I can see their souls. It’s easy to tell who is kind, who is a frightened hypocrite, and who is a predator. My partner and I moved from Kyiv to a village because of the constant shelling. I decided it would be more effective for us to live there rather than in the capital, which is under constant attack. She accepted.

I see war as a consequence of an absolutely new, naked society from all sides — one that teaches people to value life. It’s become crystal clear that only the present moment exists. Everything else is absent. Tomorrow could be completely different, or it might not come at all. It depends on what each person believes in. As they say, "To each according to their faith." The path seems straight in the eyes of man, but God weighs the heart.

I’ve realized that everything is in my hands, that reality is as subjective as I allow it to be. Over the past four years, I’ve traveled extensively across Ukraine. After my driver’s license was revoked, I began traveling everywhere without any documents, just as a sovereign human. Police, regime enforcers, and others who believe the state has the right to violence are destroying people here and turning them from a free nation into serfs.

Our forces should serve the people, but they act as if the constitution doesn’t apply to them.

How are you?
How have you been?
Are you safe?

Take care of your safety and, most importantly, peace in your heart. 🙏
Maybe this message will be the beginning of a book. Who knows?

#### **Війна як норма**

Кожен ранок в Україні починається однаково вже чотири роки поспіль. Повітряні тривоги, удари дронами, ракетами, балістикою. І це не про фронт — це про тил. На фронті все ще гірше: авіабомби, артилерія, дрони, які полюють на людей. Ми живемо в світі, де дрони полюють на людей за рахунок інших людей. Як ми до цього дійшли? Як війна стала нашою реальністю і нормою?

#### **Митці та людожери**

Є митці — ті, хто творить життя, і є людожери — ті, хто його руйнує. Людство існує як єдине ціле, і саме завдяки спільнотворчості митців ми маємо сучасну цивілізацію. Але ось парадокс: людожери користуються цією системою, яку створили митці, і водночас знищують їх. Замість того, щоб стати митцями, вони вибирають руйнування. Замість того, щоб прийняти відповідальність за своє життя, вони віддають її якомусь лідеру чи босу. А цей бос, у якого немає відповідальності за життя інших, легко кидає їх у топку війни. Обман, лицемірство, людожерство — ось як воно виглядає в максимальному прояві.

#### **Система, яка вибиває зв'язок з душею**

Війна — це наслідок. А причина — система, яка вибиває в людей зв'язок з душею. Люди, які втратили цей зв'язок, не здатні зрозуміти, що життя людини священне. Вбивати іншу людину або навіть тварину — це не просто гріх, це порушення математично чіткої формули єдності людства. Адже ми — єдине ціле. І коли хтось руйнує, він руйнує не лише життя інших, а й себе.
